1. Who we are

The MN Family app ("the app") is operated by MN Jewel Park, Vellore, Tamil Nadu, India ("MN Jewel Park", "we", "us"). We decide how personal information in the app is used, and we are responsible for it.

The app is a workplace tool. It helps our staff with daily tasks, attendance, team communication and four personal well-being "pillars": Health, Family Time, Work and Social Cause.

2. Who this policy covers

The app is only for people who work with MN Jewel Park: employees, team leads, HR, management, and outside support staff we invite. There is no public sign-up. Accounts are created by HR or management, and you sign in with the email and password they give you.

This policy also covers limited information about other people that staff enter while working, such as online-order customers, and family or friends mentioned in Family Time entries.

4. Device permissions

The app only asks for a permission when you use the feature that needs it. You can turn any of them off in your phone settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

  • Camera and photos: to take or pick a profile photo, prescription photo, Family Time or Social Cause photo, or a chat attachment.
  • Files: to attach documents and to save exported reports (PDF or Excel) when you ask.
  • Physical activity, Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone): to read your step count. See Health and step data.
  • Notifications, alarms and background running: to show reminders for tasks, attendance and announcements on time, including after your phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: to sync your data and tell you when you are offline.

5. Health and step data

If you turn on automatic steps, the app reads only your daily step count from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone). It never writes to, changes or deletes your health data there, and it reads nothing else.

  • Step counts are used only to fill in your Health pillar and the step and calorie figures shown to you.
  • Step data from Health Connect or Apple Health is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared outside MN Jewel Park.
  • You can turn automatic steps off in the app or remove the permission in Health Connect or the iPhone Health app at any time. Steps already saved stay in your Health history unless you ask us to delete them.

The use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

6. How we use information

  • To run your account, sign you in securely and help you reset a forgotten password through HR.
  • To manage attendance, leave, permissions, tasks and daily work.
  • To produce daily and monthly scorecards, team reports and exports for management.
  • To show your own progress on the Health, Family Time, Work and Social Cause pillars, and to let your team lead or management check team well-being where your role allows it.
  • To review and approve social causes, media and leave requests.
  • To send you notifications, reminders, announcements, birthday wishes and chat messages.
  • To keep the app safe, fix problems, and keep backups so data is not lost.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

7. Who can see your information

Access inside the app depends on role and on the permissions management grants:

  • You can see all of your own information.
  • Your team lead can see your team's attendance, tasks and scorecards. Where the app allows, they can also see your Health and Family Time summaries.
  • HR and assistant HR can see the staff records, attendance, leave and requests of the departments they are assigned to.
  • The CEO and management can see information across all departments, approve requests, and make company backups.
  • Other staff can see your name, photo, role and department, and anything you post in shared spaces: group chat, social causes you post, and comments on announcements.
  • Personal notes are private to you.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect the safety of staff or customers, or as part of a legal process.

9. Storage and security

  • Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored in Google Firebase.
  • Access is controlled by sign-in and by role-based security rules, so each person can only reach what their role allows.
  • Your sign-in session is kept in your phone's secure storage. Some data is also saved on your phone so the app works offline.
  • Company backups can only be made, restored or cleared by the CEO.

No system is perfectly secure. Please keep your password private and tell HR straight away if you think someone has used your account.

10. How long we keep it

  • We keep your information for as long as you work with MN Jewel Park and your account is active.
  • When you leave, HR turns off your sign-in. We keep employment, attendance and payroll-related records for as long as Indian employment, tax and other laws require, then delete them.
  • Personal pillar data (Health, Family Time, Social Cause goals) and personal notes are deleted within 90 days of your account being closed, unless the law requires us to keep them or you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Backups are overwritten in normal course.

11. Your rights and choices

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you can:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and get a summary of it.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date. You can edit much of your profile and pillar data yourself.
  • Ask us to delete information we no longer need, subject to records we must keep by law.
  • Withdraw consent for optional features, for example by turning off automatic steps, camera or notification access.
  • Name another person to act for you if you are unable to.
  • Raise a complaint with us, and if you are not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India.

Using the four pillars beyond what your job needs is optional. You may leave Health, Family Time or Social Cause entries blank.

12. Deleting your account

To delete your MN Family account and its data:

  1. Speak to HR, or email us at the address in Contact us from your registered email, with the subject "Delete my MN Family account".
  2. We confirm it is you, then turn off your sign-in straight away.
  3. Within 30 days we delete your profile, pillar data, personal notes, chat messages you sent and photos you uploaded.

We keep only the records the law requires (for example attendance and employment records), for the time the law requires, after which they are deleted.

13. Children

The app is for staff aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If a child's details are entered by mistake, for example in a Family Time note, please ask us to remove them.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. We will change the "Last updated" date above, and for important changes we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
