2. Who may use the app

3. Your account

  • HR or management creates your account. You sign in with the email and password they give you.
  • Keep your password secret. Do not share your account or let anyone else use the app as you.
  • You are responsible for what is done through your account. Tell HR straight away if you think someone else has used it.
  • If you forget your password, use "Forgot password". HR verifies your request before a new password is set.
  • Keep your profile details correct, and update them or tell HR when they change.

4. Roles and access

What you can see and do depends on your role (employee, assistant team lead, team lead, HR, assistant HR, outside support or CEO) and on the permissions management grants.

  • Use only the access you have been given, and only for its purpose. For example, use a team lead's view of the team's health or attendance only to support the team.
  • Do not try to reach screens, records or data your role does not allow, or get around access controls.
  • Management may change your role and access at any time.

5. Acceptable use

When using the app, you must not:

  • Enter false attendance, tasks, scorecard numbers, meals, steps, social services or other records, or mark work done that is not done.

  • Pillar Commitment progress may be shown to your team lead or management, as described in the Privacy Policy.

9. Health information disclaimer

The Health pillar commitment is not medical advice. BMI, calorie targets, protein, carbs, fat and fibre goals, nutrition estimates and calories burned are general estimates from standard formulas and typical food values. They may not suit your body or medical condition.

Speak to a doctor or qualified dietitian before changing your diet, exercise or medication, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition or take medicines. In an emergency, contact a doctor or emergency services, not the app.
